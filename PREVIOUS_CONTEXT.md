## 2. Fundamental Subatomic Constants & Definitions

| Particle | Charge (Coulombs) | Mass (Grams / kg) | Location / Nature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Proton ($p$)** | $+1.602 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$ | $1.672 \times 10^{-24}\text{ g}$ ($1.672 \times 10^{-27}\text{ kg}$) | Nucleus (Nucleon) |
| **Neutron ($n$)** | $0$ (Neutral) | $1.674 \times 10^{-24}\text{ g}$ ($1.674 \times 10^{-27}\text{ kg}$) | Nucleus (Nucleon) |
| **Electron ($e^-$)** | $-1.602 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$ | $9.109 \times 10^{-28}\text{ g}$ ($9.109 \times 10^{-31}\text{ kg}$) | Extra-nuclear orbitals |

### Key Nuclear Definitions:
* **Atomic Number ($Z$):** Total number of protons in the nucleus (also equal to electrons in a neutral atom).
* **Mass Number ($A$):** Total number of nucleons ($\text{Protons} + \text{Neutrons}$).
* **Neutrons:** $A - Z$.
* **Isotopes:** Same atomic number ($Z$), different mass numbers ($A$) — e.g., ${}_1^1\text{H}, {}_1^2\text{H}, {}_1^3\text{H}$.
* **Isobars:** Different atomic number ($Z$), same mass number ($A$) — e.g., ${}_{18}^{40}\text{Ar}$ and ${}_{20}^{40}\text{Ca}$.
* **Isotones:** Same number of neutrons ($A - Z$) — e.g., ${}_6^{14}\text{C}$ ($8n$) and ${}_8^{16}\text{O}$ ($8n$).

## 3. The Core Mole Concept Intuition

### The Golden Reciprocal Rule:
$$1\text{ amu} = \frac{1}{12} \text{ mass of one } {}^{12}\text{C atom} = 1.66 \times 10^{-24}\text{ g}$$
$$\text{Avogadro's Number } (N_A) = 6.022 \times 10^{23}\text{ mol}^{-1}$$
$$\mathbf{1\text{ amu} \times N_A = 1\text{ gram}}$$

* **Consequence:** An atomic mass given in $\text{amu}$ (or $\text{u}$) directly converts to the molar mass of 1 mole of those atoms in **grams**!
  * 1 atom of $\text{Na} = 23\text{ u}$
  * 1 mole of $\text{Na} = 23\text{ g}$

## 4. Fundamental Mole Formulas & Early Problem Set

1. **Moles from Mass:**
   $$n = \frac{\text{Given Mass } (w)}{\text{Molar Mass } (M)}$$
   * *Example:* Moles in $200\text{ g}$ of $\text{Ca}$ ($M = 40\text{ g/mol}$):
     $$n = \frac{200}{40} = 5\text{ moles} \implies 5 N_A \text{ atoms}$$
   * *Example:* Moles of $\text{O}_2$ molecules in $16\text{ kg}$ ($16000\text{ g}$, $M = 32\text{ g/mol}$):
     $$n = \frac{16000}{32} = 500\text{ moles}$$

2. **Ideal Gas Equation & STP Volume:**
   $$PV = nRT$$
   * At STP ($T = 273.15\text{ K}, P = 1\text{ atm}$):
     $$\mathbf{1\text{ mole of any gas} = 22.4\text{ Liters}}$$

3. **Relative Atomic Mass (RAM):**
   $$\text{RAM} = \frac{\text{Mass of 1 atom of element}}{\frac{1}{12} \times \text{Mass of 1 atom of } {}^{12}\text{C}}$$
   * **RAM is a dimensionless ratio (pure number with NO units).**
